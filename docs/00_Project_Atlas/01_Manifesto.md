## Our Belief

We believe supply chains are stronger when businesses work together.

We believe unused capacity is wasted opportunity.

We believe technology should connect every participant in the supply chain, regardless of size.

We believe collaboration creates greater value than isolation.

We believe every truck, warehouse, pallet position, and future supply chain resource should be discoverable, accessible, and capable of generating value.

We believe the future of supply chains is not built on bigger networks. It is built on better connections.

**That is why Froto exists.**

## A Founder's Manifesto

The world's supply chains are more connected than ever, yet the businesses that power them remain surprisingly disconnected.

Every day, transport companies run trucks with empty space. Warehouses hold unused capacity while other businesses struggle to find storage. Manufacturers, distributors, retailers, and logistics providers spend countless hours making phone calls, sending emails, and searching personal networks in the hope of finding the right capacity at the right time.

Too often, opportunities are missed, costs increase, and profitability suffers, not because capacity does not exist, but because it cannot be easily discovered.

After more than twenty years working in supply chain, I saw the same problems repeated. Technology continued to evolve, yet one of the industry's most fundamental challenges remained unchanged. Finding transport or warehouse capacity still depended on who you knew, how many calls you could make, or how much extra you were prepared to pay when time was running out.

The industry's knowledge lived in people's heads instead of within a connected marketplace.

That experience led to a simple but ambitious question:

> **Why doesn't the supply chain have a true spot market?**

Financial markets allow buyers and sellers to trade instantly. Energy markets balance supply and demand in real time. Consumers can book accommodation, transport, and services through digital marketplaces in seconds.

Yet one of the world's largest industries still relies heavily on spreadsheets, email, phone calls, and personal relationships to discover available capacity.

Froto exists to change that.

Our vision is to build the world's first spot marketplace for supply chain capacity, connecting every participant through a single platform where available transport, warehouse capacity, and future supply chain services can be discovered, traded, and managed in real time.

We believe every unused truck, every empty pallet position, and every available warehouse location represents unrealised value. Capacity should never remain invisible simply because businesses cannot find one another.

Froto is built on a simple belief: when businesses collaborate, the entire supply chain becomes stronger. By creating a trusted marketplace where organisations of every size can connect, utilise spare capacity, and build new commercial relationships, we help reduce waste, lower operating costs, and improve profitability for everyone involved.

Our ambition extends beyond building software.

We are building the infrastructure that allows supply chains to collaborate at scale.

Success for Froto will not be measured only by revenue or growth. It will be measured by the day businesses create dedicated roles or teams whose purpose is to maximise value through the Froto spot market, and by the moment the industry no longer asks whether a company uses Froto, but how effectively it uses it.

This is more than a product.

It is a long-term commitment to changing how the world's supply chains discover capacity, work together, and create value.

This is the beginning of that journey.
