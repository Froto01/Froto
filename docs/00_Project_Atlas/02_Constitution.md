# The Froto Constitution

**Founder:** David Yardley  
**Company:** Froto  
**Document series:** Project Atlas  
**Status:** Approved foundation  
**Version:** 1.0

The Froto Constitution defines the principles that guide company, product, commercial, and technical decisions. It is intended to remain stable even as features, technology, and market conditions evolve.

## VIS-001: Vision

> Froto exists to become the world's first spot marketplace for supply chain capacity, connecting every participant in the supply chain to buy, sell, and optimise logistics and storage capacity in real time.

## MIS-001: Mission

> Increase utilisation across the global supply chain, reducing waste, lowering costs, and improving profitability for every participant.

## PRO-001: Product Promise

> Every interaction on Froto should help a business find, utilise, or manage supply chain capacity more efficiently than they could using email, spreadsheets, or phone calls.

## OBJ-001: Six-Month Objective

> Within six months, Froto is robust enough to onboard its first pilot customer and support their day-to-day logistics workflow.

## MKT-001: Ultimate Market

Froto is ultimately designed for every participant in the supply chain.

## MKT-002: Initial Pilot Customer

The first pilot customer profile is an Australian transport company or warehouse operator with:

- 5 to 50 vehicles and/or 1 to 3 warehouse sites
- spare transport or storage capacity
- customer enquiries currently managed through email, spreadsheets, and phone calls
- a need to improve utilisation, quoting speed, visibility, and commercial outcomes

## MKT-003: Balanced Marketplace

Froto will build both sides of the marketplace from the beginning.

### Supply

- Transport providers
- Warehouse providers

### Demand

- Manufacturers
- Importers
- Exporters
- Retailers
- Wholesalers
- Distributors
- Third-party logistics providers

## GR-001: Monetise Unused Capacity

Every unused piece of supply chain capacity should be capable of being monetised through Froto.

## GR-002: Create Measurable Value

Every feature must increase utilisation, reduce friction, or improve profitability.

## GR-003: Simplicity Beats Complexity

Froto should make difficult supply chain workflows easier to understand and complete. Complexity must earn its place.

## GR-004: Build Trust Before Scale

Verification, transparency, reliability, clear rules, and accountable marketplace behaviour are prerequisites for growth.

## GR-005: Strengthen Both Sides

Every release must make Froto more valuable for both buyers and sellers of capacity.

## PP-001: Product First

Technology decisions must follow validated product and customer needs, not novelty.

## PP-002: Simple Wins

The simplest complete workflow is preferred over a larger collection of partially solved features.

## PP-003: Commercial Thinking

Froto must create real economic value for users and a sustainable business model for the company.

## PP-004: Scale From Day One

The architecture and operating model should avoid preventable barriers to future growth, while not overengineering the pilot product.

## PP-005: Quality Over Quantity

A smaller number of reliable, coherent workflows is more valuable than a wide but fragile feature set.

## PP-006: Marketplace First

Features should reinforce discovery, trading, management, or optimisation of supply chain capacity.

## PP-007: Customer First

Product decisions must be grounded in real customer workflows, pains, risks, and commercial outcomes.

## PP-008: Documentation First

Major decisions, requirements, rules, and changes should be documented alongside the product and code.

## PP-009: Security by Design

Security, privacy, access control, auditability, and data protection are product requirements, not late-stage additions.

## PP-010: Scalable Architecture

Technical choices should support measured growth, maintainability, integration, and operational visibility without sacrificing delivery speed.

## DP-001: Collaboration at Scale

> Competition creates winners and losers. Collaboration creates stronger supply chains. Froto exists to enable collaboration at scale.

Froto does not remove competition. It creates a trusted environment where businesses can collaborate around unused capacity for mutually beneficial outcomes.

## DECISION TEST

Before approving a major feature, partnership, workflow, or technical investment, Froto asks:

1. Does it support the vision?
2. Does it solve a meaningful customer problem?
3. Does it increase utilisation, reduce friction, improve trust, or create commercial value?
4. Does it strengthen the marketplace for both supply and demand?
5. Is it the simplest responsible solution?

If the answer is not clear, the decision should be challenged, refined, deferred, or rejected.

## CHANGE CONTROL

Changes to this Constitution must be recorded in the Project Atlas Decision Log with:

- the principle or statement changed
- the reason for the change
- the evidence supporting it
- the expected impact
- the approval date
